[00:03:10] SARAH:
Let's talk about video retention. Everyone is trying to crack YouTube Shorts and TikTok right now. Why do so many clips fall flat?

[00:03:22] KAMRAJ:
We audited over 1,000 short-form clips. 73% of viewers consume short-form video on mobile with the sound completely off. If your subtitles are slow, tiny, or placed in the platform 'dead zone' where the like, comment, and share buttons sit on the right side, the viewer swipes away in 1.8 seconds. Retention is an eye-tracking game. Your subtitles need to move in dynamic 2-to-3 word kinetic bursts with contrasting highlights. Furthermore, your first 2 seconds must challenge a belief or quantify a loss. If your hook says 'Hey guys, today I want to share a tip', you have already lost 80% of your audience.

[00:05:15] SARAH:
What about speech-to-text accuracy? A lot of people think all AI speech models are identical.

[00:05:22] KAMRAJ:
That is a massive misconception. If you are discussing general lifestyle topics, standard ASR is fine. But when you are a B2B creator discussing 'CreatorOps', 'LTV:CAC', or 'ASR latency', generic models introduce phonetic errors. When a mobile viewer sees a misspelled technical word, their reading flow stumbles for 200 milliseconds, and that cognitive friction causes an immediate swipe. You need domain-specific vocabulary whitelisting and sub-millisecond audio synchronization.
