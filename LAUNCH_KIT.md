## 8. Risk Management & Content Moderation

| Risk Scenario | Likelihood | Impact | Preventive Protocol & Rollback Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Social API Auth Token Expiration** | Medium | High | Automated ping script checks OAuth status every 7 days. Buffer alerts trigger 72 hours prior to expiration. |
| **Negative Comment or Troll Attack** | Low | Low | Rule: Never argue or delete constructive criticism. Respond with data and invitation to test tool free. Hide spam or hate speech immediately. |
| **Incorrect Technical Claim or ASR Typo** | Low | Medium | Pre-publish checklist requires human verification of all numbers and names against the source recording. If an error is published, pin an errata comment immediately. |
| **Algorithm Reach Slump** | Medium | Medium | If organic reach drops >30% over 7 days, trigger the "Pattern Interrupt Protocol": switch from formal carousels to raw split-screen founder demos and increase X thread frequency. |
